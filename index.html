About KALAI LINK

Millions of marginalized artisans in India possess incredible traditional skills, but lack the digital literacy, market linkages, and presentation needed to compete in modern e-commerce.

Our mission is to democratize digital commerce for rural artisans. We leverage Artificial Intelligence to act as a virtual business manager—connecting raw talent directly to retail and corporate buyers worldwide.
